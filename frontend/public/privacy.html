Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

This policy explains what information Editorial receives, why it is used, and when it is shared. Editorial is a document collaboration service available at editorial.justshivamm.in.

Information we handle

  • Account information: your email address and the basic account details supplied by the sign-in provider. When you choose Google sign-in, Clerk manages that sign-in and Editorial uses your verified primary email to identify your workspace account. Editorial does not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail, or contacts.
  • Workspace content: documents, drafts, comments, suggestions, attachments, invitations, tags, deadlines, and related activity that you or your collaborators add.
  • Technical information: session information, connection and error logs, and data needed to keep real-time editing working. The browser may store session and workspace preferences locally.

How we use information

We use this information to sign you in, provide and secure the workspace, synchronize editing, apply document permissions, send requested collaboration notices, troubleshoot problems, and improve the service. If you choose a writing assistance feature, the relevant text is sent to the configured AI provider to produce a response.

When information is shared

Content is visible to the people you invite according to their access role. If a document owner creates a public read-only link, anyone with that link may view the shared document until the link expires or is revoked. Workspace owners can also configure outbound integrations that send selected document events to destinations they choose.

Service providers may process information to operate Editorial, including Clerk for authentication, Google for Google sign-in, Supabase for data storage, Vercel and Render for hosting, EmailJS for configured email delivery, and the configured AI provider for requested AI features. Each provider receives information needed for its part of the service. We do not sell personal information or use Google sign-in data for advertising.

Storage, retention, and security

Workspace data is stored while it is needed to provide the service. Deleted documents may remain temporarily in backups or operational records. Account and content deletion requests can be sent to the contact below; we will review them alongside any content that other collaborators need to retain and applicable legal obligations. We use access controls and authenticated connections, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can choose what to put in a document, whom to invite, whether to create a public link, and whether to use optional AI or integrations. You can stop using Google sign-in or revoke its connection through your Google account settings. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information.

Children and changes

Editorial is intended for people using a collaborative work or study workspace and is not directed to children. We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above will show the latest published version.

Contact
